Our story

A casual family Italian restaurant since 1993

Carmine's opened its doors in 1993. A fire in 2015 changed our course, and we reopened in East Amherst in 2017 before finding our forever home here at 7170 Transit Road in September 2022. Family owned and operated by Kelly & Alex Jacobbi.
